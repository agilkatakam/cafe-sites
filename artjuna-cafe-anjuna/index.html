Artjuna garden hall

A Mediterranean table in Anjuna, since 2005.

Grown, not built.

It began in 2005 with two tables and eight chairs under the trees. Today it is one long arched hall of cane chairs, collected art, a bakery, a bar and a book corner. The garden still does most of the hosting.

Rated 4.2 from over ten thousand Google reviews. Open every day of the year, 7:00 to 22:30.
