The salon

Vipul Chudasama's name has been part of Indian hairdressing for over two decades - first as a hairdresser, then as the founder of a salon, an academy and a consultancy.

He has led more than 600 hair shows, seminars and masterclasses, and trained over 60,000 hairdressers worldwide. The Pali Hill salon is where that craft meets the chair.

Makeup is led by Pooja Chudasama, whose work spans fashion, editorial, film, television and bridal - with an eye for colour and skin that keeps brides coming back by referral.

Every guest is treated as a favourite, and every product on the shelf is professional grade. Come and experience it.
