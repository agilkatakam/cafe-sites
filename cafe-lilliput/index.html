From a beach shack, 1986.

In the heady days of the eighties, when Goa meant freedom and exploration, Lilliput was born - a gathering place for wanderers, dreamers and free spirits on Anjuna's sand.

The shack witnessed the birth of a global community drawn to the beach, the music and the magic of Goa - and gave the coastline its soundtrack, from hypnotic techno to soul-soothing rhythms.

Four decades on, the room has modernised and the essence has held: sit by the shore, let the breeze do its work, and stay as long as the night asks you to. Come find the deck.
