Music seeps into your meal

The courtyard canteen of Triveni Kala Sangam, Mandi House

Galleries and theatres share the courtyard

An adda since 1963

What began as a stove and a table for dancers after rehearsal is now Delhi’s beloved courtyard canteen: chai in kulhars, kebabs on the griddle, conversations that outlast the cups.

Joseph Allen Stein planned Triveni around its courtyard like a village square. You cannot cross it without passing a rehearsal, an exhibition or a performance.
