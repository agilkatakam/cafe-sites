Strength Gym is a multifunctional gym in Khar West, Mumbai, built on one idea: training should welcome every fitness level.

The floor covers strength training and cardio. The pool runs swimming lessons and aqua rehab therapy. Steam, sauna and an ice bath take care of recovery.

Members train with a plan: experienced coaches, personal training, and a nutrition plan when they want one.

A second branch trains at the Ramee Guestline Hotel in Juhu. Rated 4.8 from 136 Google reviews.

Questions

Where is Strength Gym? Two branches in Mumbai: Ramee Techome, plot 786–787, 3rd Road, Govind Dham, Khar West 400052, and the Ramee Guestline Hotel, 462 AB Nair Road, Juhu 400049.

What are the timings? Hours are not listed online; call +91 98679 11577 and the team will confirm today's schedule.

Is there really a swimming pool? Yes. The pool runs recreational swimming, swimming lessons and aqua rehab therapy.

Do you offer personal training? Yes, one-to-one sessions with a trainer, backed by a training plan and a nutrition plan.

What about recovery? Steam, sauna and an ice bath sit beside the gym floor, so a session can end properly.

Is it beginner friendly? That is the gym's own promise: a welcoming, inclusive environment for all fitness levels.

Do you run yoga? Yoga is listed among the gym's offerings; ask the front desk for the current class schedule.

How do I join? Call +91 98679 11577, message on WhatsApp, or email info@strengthgym.in.
