a small clinic, round like a mirror

Every dentist starts a visit with a small round mirror. It shows the part of your mouth you cannot see for yourself. That is the idea behind a good visit: look properly first, explain what is there, then decide on treatment together.

You will find it on Bazar Street in Neelasandra, Shanti Nagar, directly below Canara Bank and opposite Mecca Mosque.
