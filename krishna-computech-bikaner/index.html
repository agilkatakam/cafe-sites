Tell the workshop

The screen side

Cracked panels, flickering display cables, hinges that snap the lid shut: the parts you touch every day, replaced with quality spares and fitted clean.

Power and heat

Adapters, batteries, DC jacks. Fans cleaned or replaced, old thermal paste renewed. These are the fixes that stop a laptop shutting itself down in a Bikaner summer.

The board

Motherboard and PC repairs down to component level: micro-soldering stations, BIOS programming, dead ports, RAM upgrades. Specialised diagnostic equipment finds the fault before a single part is ordered.

Data and beyond

Data recovery from drives that won't spin up. Keyboards, DVD-RW drives, printer repair and CCTV security. Hardware and software troubleshooting under one roof.
