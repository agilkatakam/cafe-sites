The label

Shreeda Couture is a women's boutique in Paschim Vihar, Delhi. The cloth is chosen, cut and stitched in the label's own manufacturing unit in West Delhi, so a design moves from table to rail without leaving the house.

Ordering

Walk in and pick from the rails in sizes 38 to 44, or sit with the team and have a dress stitched to order. Fabric, style and timeline are settled with you before the first cut.
