The chef

Vandana Naik cooked for nearly two decades in New York, trained at the Culinary Institute of America, and ran one of Goa's favourite cafe brands before opening Bodega, her first restaurant.

Her food is the kind that makes unfamiliar dishes feel like comfort food the first time you eat them. Aretha Franklin called her desserts the best she had ever had; her cakes have travelled to tables that include Beyonce's and Ben Kingsley's.

Here she cooks quieter: soups, salads, sandwiches and cakes, baked fresh every day and served in the shade of an art centre courtyard. See the menu.
