Budha Halwai

A sweet shop in Nashik, famous for its jalebi since 1957.

A sweet counter behind Bhadrakali Mandir that Nashik has queued at for three generations.

Call the counter

Budha Halwai began in 1957, and its jalebi turned into the taste the city points at first. Hot, crisp coils come off the kadhai through the day, and the counter does a steady trade in pedhas, ladoos and samosas alongside.

The shop stands in the old lanes of Naikwadi Pura, a few steps behind Bhadrakali Mandir, much as it always has.

From the counter

  • Jalebi, fried through the day, sold hot
  • Pakka Pedha, the shop’s own recipe
  • Raghavdas Ladoo, a Nashik favourite
  • Special Dry Samosa, crisp and savoury
  • Seasonal mithai, as the calendar turns

The shop and its sweets

Three generations of Nashik have grown up on these plates. The recipe has not moved, and neither has the shop.
