Tsuki is a pan-Asian restaurant in Koregaon Park, Pune. Thai curries, Cantonese dim sum, sushi and a charcoal robata grill, served in a plant-filled conservatory with a full bar.

Evenings are dinner-only; lunch runs Friday to Sunday. The room glows after dark, which is very much the point.
