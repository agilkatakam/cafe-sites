The Story

Thirty seven years and counting

In 1989, when Vizag still dressed for tradition, two brothers opened a small shop of western wear near Jagadamba Junction. People laughed at clothes they did not understand.

Then cable TV arrived, the city wanted what its stars wore, and Dolls N Chic was already there.

Dilip Bulchandani picks the clothing himself on buying trips to Mumbai and Delhi, the way he has since the beginning. Kamal Bulchandani curates the footwear. Their father ran Moti Creations in One Town; the brothers took the trade west.

The shop opened with about 150 designs of footwear and 40 garments. It grew into more than 900 designs, heaped on tables and hung from every available surface, in the same corner premises it started in.

The regulars never changed either. Generations of Vizag shoppers walk in knowing they will leave with something different, at prices a student can manage.
