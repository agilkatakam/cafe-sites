General Tree and Lawn Service of Collier is a family company in Naples, Florida. It has cared for the trees and lawns of Collier County since 1985.

A certified arborist is on staff. The crew trims and removes trees of every size, from a palm over the driveway to an old oak over the roof.

The lawn side keeps grass cut, beds planted and whole landscapes maintained, for homes and for businesses.

No job is too big or too small. Call or email and ask for a free estimate.
