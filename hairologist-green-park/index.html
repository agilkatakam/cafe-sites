The work 29 frames from the floor

Hairologist is a hair salon built like a clinic and run like a couture house, in Green Park Market, New Delhi.

Founder and creative director Sameer leads a floor of master stylists and trichologists. Indu Chaudhary heads hair patch and extensions; Pragati Chanshouliya leads beauty and makeup.

The salon is known for medical-grade work: natural hair patches and micro-ring extensions, and ultra-gentle cranial prostheses for chemotherapy patients, fitted in private.

VIP styling suites, shiatsu wash lounges and a custom colour bar back up the everyday cuts, colour, keratin and bridal work.

Open seven days, 9:30 am to 9:00 pm. Read the Google reviews.
