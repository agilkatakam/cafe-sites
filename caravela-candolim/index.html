Four cafés across Goa

Panjim, Candolim, Margao and Varca. Candolim was the second.

Roastery and bakery in-house

Latin Quarter coffee, and every loaf of bread, made by the café itself.

The first café in Goa with its own coffee roasters.

Caravela’s own roastery brand is called Latin Quarter, after the Panjim neighbourhood where it all began. The must-try is the Latin Quarter Robusta · shade-grown, with spice and citrus notes, taken to a light french roast.

  • Roasted by the café itself · the first in Goa to do it.
  • Latin Quarter Robusta · shade-grown, spice and citrus, light french roast.
  • Every bread on the menu comes out of the in-house bakery.
  • Ask your server what’s special today · it changes with the ovens.
