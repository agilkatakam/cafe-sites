A baking school that opened a café

The academy

Founded in 2012 by chefs Vinesh Johny, Avin Thaliath and Lijo Chandy, Lavonne began as the Lavonne Academy of Baking Science & Pastry Arts - a school for the craft, before it was ever a café.

The café

The Indiranagar café was the first. Today there are five across Bengaluru and one in New Delhi, and the Times Food & Nightlife Awards have named Lavonne Best Café eight times between 2016 and 2024, in the café's own telling.
