Tattoo Cafe

The place

Three floors above Badi Chaupar, opposite the Hawa Mahal, sits a cafe with ink in its veins. The kitchen runs cafe, Italian and North Indian lines; the counter pours chai, juices and shakes. Up on the terrace the palace fills the frame, and in the in-house studio professional artists tattoo while the city hums below.

30, 3rd Floor, Rang Bihari Temple, Badi Chaupar, opposite Hawa Mahal, Jaipur 302002.
