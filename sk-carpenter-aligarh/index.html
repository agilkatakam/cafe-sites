Sk Carpenter & Furniture

Chairs and cots, kitchen furniture, varnishing and repairs - one workshop in Jamalpur, Aligarh.

98973 51236 skcrazywood@gmail.com

The workshop makes and mends wooden furniture: chairs, cots and beds, kitchen units, and the small jobs in between - a sliding window that sticks, a chair that wobbles, a cot that needs taking apart and putting back together in a new room.

Its public listing describes a team with years of experience in the carpentry field that prioritises work suited to the customer's budget, from renovating apartment woodwork to modular kitchens and polishing - those are the listing's own words, not ours.
