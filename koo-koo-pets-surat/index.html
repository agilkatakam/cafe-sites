Why Koo Koo

Koo Koo is a pet shop and veterinary clinic at Silver Point, on Jivanjyot Circle in Puna Gam, Surat.

Grooming, supplies and the clinic sit under one roof, so a bath, a bag of food and a checkup can all happen in one visit.

Reviewers mention the polite staff, the fair prices and the well stocked shelves.

The clinic also runs free checkups for street animals, which reviewers mention most warmly of all.
