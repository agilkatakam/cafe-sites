Jaipur’s blue room

A pure vegetarian multi-cuisine cafe at The White Moon, Shyam Nagar. Italian, Mexican and Indian plates, coffee all day.

Call +91 98290 17934

Cafesa is a newly built, pure vegetarian cafe inside The White Moon hotel in Shyam Nagar, Jaipur. The room is modern and artistic, with a heritage look, and it is painted a deep, quiet blue.

The kitchen moves between Italian, Mexican and Indian cooking. Pastas and aglio olio sit next to Manchurian, paneer tikka and a special thali. Coffee, mocktails and shakes run all day, with waffles and brownies for the sweet end of the table.

Guests on TripAdvisor single out the Gulabi Nagri Pasta, the pink-sauce pasta and the chilli garlic noodles, and rate the cafe among Jaipur’s best new tables. Google shows 4.7 from about 1,465 reviews.

Families, coffee dates and long four-hour dinners all fit here. Find the cafe.
