The shop

Cake WALK is a small bakery in Hinoo, Ranchi, known around the neighbourhood for its made to order cakes.

Birthdays, anniversaries and small family celebrations get cakes built to the occasion, from red velvet to chocolate drip, number cakes and doll cakes for kids.

The counter carries fresh pastries and slices through the day, and the shop is rated 4.6 out of 5 on Google.

You will find us on Hinoo Main Road, near Eyelex. Call ahead and we will set something aside for you. See us on the map.
