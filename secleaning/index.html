SE Cleaning Services is a Cork cleaning company with over sixteen years behind it. Offices, shops, homes and industrial units across Munster and Leinster, cleaned by fully insured staff at hours that suit the building.

Sixteen years in. A Cork based cleaning company that has built its name on steady, consistent work for commercial, industrial and residential customers.

Every kind of building. Offices and shops, homes and rentals, industrial units and end of tenancy cleans. No job is too small, and larger contracts are welcome too.

Based in Cork city. Working from Commercial Road, Cork T12 H9CF, and covering clients across the Munster and Leinster districts.

Ring or email. Call 085 247 5054 or write to secleaning22@gmail.com for a quote. Early, late and out of hours work can be arranged to suit your building.
