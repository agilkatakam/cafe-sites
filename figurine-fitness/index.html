The founder

Santosh turned a passion for rhythm and sport into India's first dance-fitness studio in the late 1980s. A national jazz champion and internationally qualified expert, he has trained over three lakh members and 400 instructors.

The method

With his wife Anu and a team of 15-plus trainers, he leads dance fitness, gym training, rehabilitation and nutrition across Bangalore. No judgments: they only care that you show up.
