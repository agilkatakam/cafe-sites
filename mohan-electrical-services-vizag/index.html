A neighbourhood electrician

Mohan Electrical Services is a small electrical shop in Chaitanya Nagar, Gajuwaka, Visakhapatnam. The work is the everyday kind: wiring and repairs, outlets and panels, fans, fittings and the circuits behind them.

Who. A local electrician listed for residential electrical service in Gajuwaka. Calls come in for the small jobs and the urgent ones: a fan that stopped mid-afternoon, a socket that gave up, wiring for a new room.

What. The listed services are residential electrical service, electrical outlets, electrical panels, appliance circuits, and smoke detectors with surge protection. Repairs, replacements and fresh installations, done at your place.

Where. Chinagantyada, Chaitanya Nagar, Gajuwaka, Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh 530026. A residential pocket of one of the city's busiest suburbs, inland from the port and the beach road.

Reach. One call sorts most things: +91 97011 24206. Or write to karnammohanmohan@gmail.com. Listed hours: Monday to Saturday 8am to 10pm, Sunday 9am to 5pm; please confirm when you call.
