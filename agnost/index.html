fair questions

Should a specialist replace frontier models?

Not everywhere. Agnost says this is one model for one workload. Frontier models stay the right choice for open-ended work.

How do I send traces?

Through OpenTelemetry or the Agnost SDK.

When would I switch models?

Only when the specialist wins on your own workload, measured on success, latency and cost.

What does Agnost need from me?

If you have meaningful daily traffic, an anonymized trace export. Agnost builds a held-out evaluation set and tells you whether a specialist is worth training.
