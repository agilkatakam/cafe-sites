A neighbourhood bakery

Bakers Castle sits on the ground floor of Siripuram Towers, in the middle of Visakhapatnam's shopping mile. The counter runs on cakes made to order for birthdays and occasions, with fresh bakes through the day and a few tables for anyone staying for a coffee.

The board outside lists the range: pizzas, burgers, sandwiches, breads, pastries, desserts, chocolates, brownies and milk shakes. Most cakes are built to order, so call a day ahead and tell the counter what the occasion is.
