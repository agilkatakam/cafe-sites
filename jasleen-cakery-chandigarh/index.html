Jasleen Cakery is a home bakery in Sector 44, Chandigarh. Every cake is baked to order, never pulled from a shelf.

Birthdays, anniversaries, engagements and weddings: tell us the occasion and the date, and the cake is designed around it.

The workshop's signature is the working barrel cake, built to pour. Numbered tiers, fresh florals and hand set toppers fill the rest of the order book.

To order, call or write to us with your date.
