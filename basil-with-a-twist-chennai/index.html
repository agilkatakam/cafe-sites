A twist on the European table

Basil with a Twist is a women-owned European restaurant on Habibullah Road in T. Nagar, Chennai, with a second room on OMR in Sholinganallur.

The kitchen moves between Italian and continental classics: hand-tossed nine-inch thin crusts, pastas in pink, white and pesto sauces, slow arborio risottos, and Thai curries from its Around the World pages.

The house keeps signatures of its own. Candlelight dinners on request, cakes and bakes made for the occasion, and vegan or no-onion-no-garlic plates cooked to order for anyone who asks.

More than three thousand diners have rated the T. Nagar room 4.3 stars. Ask your host about the soup of the day when you call.
