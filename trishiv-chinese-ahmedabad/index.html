Chinese kitchen and cafe, University Area, Ahmedabad.

A Chinese kitchen and cafe opposite Yanki Sizzler, in the University Area, keeps its tables set into the evening. The listing counts more than two hundred and fifty dishes and mocktails, sixteen soups alone, and mentions live music with dinner.
