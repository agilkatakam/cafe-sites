About Regal

Bold walls, big plates, one regal appetite

The Regal Cafe & Restro is a cafe and restaurant on Nahar Road in Bhatagaon, right in front of Urmila Hospital, with a mural painted dining room.

The menu covers breakfast combos, thalis, dosas and burgers. Dine in, or get your order delivered.
