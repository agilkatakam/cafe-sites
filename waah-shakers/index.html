The room

Pink walls, wooden tables, a coffee bar up front and a turf garden out back. The wall by the door says it: coffee is always a good idea.

Good to know

  • Live music evenings - the cafe posts its lineup on Facebook.

  • Garden seating out back, family tables inside.
  • Breakfast, lunch and dinner - Maggi to thali to pizza.
