Ganesh Garage is a motorcycle repair shop on Champasari Main Road in Siliguri. Engine work, diagnostics and electrics, with genuine parts.

The garage. A team of experienced mechanics who work fast, take pride in the job, and guide you in looking after your bike. Genuine parts and accessories are used throughout.

The work. Engine repair and service, oil changes, vehicle diagnostics, charging and battery checkups, spark plugs, filters, coolant, transmission oil, chain lubrication, side stand fitting and choke operation.

The address. Number 4, Champasari Main Road, Narmada Bagan Road, Champasari, Siliguri 734003. Open Monday to Saturday, 9am to 9pm; Sundays 9am to 3pm.

Getting in touch. Call 98325 90001 or write to sah2857@gmail.com. They always try to have your bike back on the road the same day; bigger repairs take as long as they take.
