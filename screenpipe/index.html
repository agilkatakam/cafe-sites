Questions.

What is screenpipe?

screenpipe records screen activity and audio so your AI can use your work history as context. It runs on macOS, Windows, and Linux, with a local API and MCP server for connecting your agents.

Does my history leave my computer?

Raw history stays local by default.

Can a company roll it out?

Manage devices, identity, policies, storage, and audit trails from one enterprise workspace.

How do I keep sensitive work out?

Exclude apps, windows, and URLs, then redact sensitive fields on your device.

Which tools can it connect to?

Connect your tools once, then make them available to the workflows you approve.
