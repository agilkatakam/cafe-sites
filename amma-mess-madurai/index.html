The story

Amma Mess is Madurai's famous non veg table. It serves Chettinad food on Alagar Kovil Main Road in Tallakulam, with a second room in KK Nagar.

The story begins in 1936, when Manickam, a cook returned from Burma, and his wife Amma Thai started a street stall with no name and no menu in Tallakulam.

In 1992 their son Senthilvel and his wife Sumathi broke off from the family business to start Amma Mess. It has been Madurai's meat landmark since.

The card runs wide: chicken, mutton, fish, crab and prawn, and game like quail, rabbit, pigeon and turkey.

Ask anyone what to order first. They will say the bone marrow omelette.
