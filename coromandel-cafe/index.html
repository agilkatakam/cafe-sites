A villa that kept its ceilings

Coromandel Café lives inside La Maison Rose, a restored French-colonial house in White Town: fifteen-foot ceilings, chandeliers, a white verandah with wicker chairs, and a green courtyard just off the promenade.

The kitchen bakes through the day: breads, cakes and a dessert counter with daily specials, and keeps a thoughtful line of vegan plates alongside the continental menu.

Come early for South Indian coffee on the verandah, or late for pizza and something cold as the street lamps come on.
