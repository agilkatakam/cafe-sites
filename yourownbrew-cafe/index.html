Your Own Brew

Freshly brewed coffee and scrumptious food, all day, every day.

See the menu

A coffee shop on S Rajmohalla in Indore where the brew is the point. Lose yourself in the aroma of a fresh pour, munch on your favourite plates and linger in the coziest corner of the neighbourhood.

Regulars know it simply as YOB.

From the kitchen

Coffee first, then a menu that runs from cafe classics to full North Indian plates.

  • Hara Bhara Kebab (veg)spinach and pea patties, green chutney
  • Paneer Lababdar (veg)rich tomato gravy, fresh cream
  • Kaaju Curry (veg)cashews in a slow simmered sauce
  • Daal Makhani (veg)black lentils, butter, patience
  • Arrabiata Pasta (veg)garlic, chilli, tomato
  • Chole Bhature (veg)the Indore favourite, done right
  • Oreo Shakecold, thick, unapologetic

The room

Tucked into Raj Mohalla, a short hop from Bombay Bazaar, YOB is built for slowing down. Come for a quick filter coffee, stay for a plate of kebabs and another round.

Tag your table with #YOBCafe when you stop by.

All day, every day

Morning brews, working lunches, late shakes. The kitchen and the coffee run through the day, so there is no wrong hour to walk in.

Call ahead for groups and celebrations on +91 93999 59259.
