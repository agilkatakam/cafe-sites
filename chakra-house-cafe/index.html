About Chakra House

Yogic food in the heart of Gokulam

Chakra House Cafe sits on 1st A Main Road in Gokulam, Mysore. It has served healthy yogic food since 2002, with a hand painted Shiva mural on the wall and a chalkboard of the seven chakras by the door.

The kitchen is vegetarian, with Indian plates, teas and light cafe fare on the menu.
