EDDIE’SPatisserie & coffee shop · Chakrata Road, Dehradun

Above the bank, a Dehradun fixture

Eddie’s Patisserie & Coffee Shop sits on the first floor above the Punjab National Bank branch on Chakrata Road, in Yamuna Colony. The counter is self-service: cakes, pastries and cup-cakes lined up behind the glass, coffee brewed hot and cold at the other end.

Inside it is tiled wood and glass tables; outside, a row of tables in the sun among pots of seasonal flowers. There is a take-out menu, a shelf of board games for anyone staying a while, and a TripAdvisor Travelers’ Choice award on the window.
