We are an open-air cafe and restaurant under a glass canopy on VIP Road in Purena, Raipur, with plants overhead and green on every side.

The kitchen runs across cafe food, Continental, Chinese, North Indian, Italian and desserts, and we cater events off site too. Most plates sit in the Rs 600 for two range.
