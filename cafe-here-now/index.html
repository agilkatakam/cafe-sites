The letter

Cafe Here & Now is a cozy literary escape and artisan cafe in HSR Layout, Sector 6 - a peaceful sanctuary tucked away on 5th Main Road.

Inside: curated books, comforting continental dishes and premium specialty brews. It is a second-floor getaway built to unplug, read, and dine at your own pace.

Guests come for the old-school English breakfast, stay for the comics and the coloured pens, and leave their screens alone.
