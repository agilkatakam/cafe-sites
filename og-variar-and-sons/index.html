O.G. Variar and Sons is a bakery in Rajajinagar, Bengaluru. You will find it on 12th Main Road, behind ESI Hospital, opposite the Anjaneya Swamy Temple.

Twelve thousand four hundred and thirty-eight guests have rated it on Google, and it holds a 4.5.
