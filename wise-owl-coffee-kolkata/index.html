Why people stay a while

Ten years on the same street

The cafe is celebrating ten years on the same street, still women-owned, still on Purna Das Road. More than five thousand eight hundred diners have rated it 4.1 stars.

Breakfast all day

The All Day Breakfast Platter runs from opening to close: two fried eggs, sausages, fries, bacon and toast for ₹290. Omelettes and pancakes alongside.

Shakes in a jar

Kitkat, Brownie, Butterscotch and eight more shakes in a jar, from ₹140. Fruit mojitos and a Sparkling Espresso for the coffee side.

Behind a white picket fence

The shopfront sits back from the pavement behind plants and a picket fence, a few doors from Azad Hind Dhaba. Their own words: more than just a coffee experience.
