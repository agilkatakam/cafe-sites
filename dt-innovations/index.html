About DT Innovations

DT Innovations Projects and Infra builds swimming pools from the excavation up: RCC shells, readymade FRP pools, mosaic murals, filtration and underwater lights, then keeps the water right with chemicals and annual care.

The workshop. Led by Dnyanesh Thuse, a pool builder with more than thirty years in the trade, with its own FRP manufacturing unit for tanks, ladders, channels and statues.

The work. Design, RCC and FRP construction, glass mosaic tiles and CAD murals, filtration systems, lights, waterproofing, chemicals and maintenance.

The reach. Based in Pune and building in Nashik, Goa, Udaipur, Kolhapur and Karnataka, with project delivery across India.

The contact. Call or WhatsApp +91 95277 45305 or +91 92725 25305, or write to dtinnovations2@gmail.com.
