Mahadev Auto Services is a two-wheeler workshop at Sapphire Elegance, Shantinagar 2, opposite the water tank in Rajkot. The team handles minor and major servicing, engine, brake and electrical repairs, wheel alignment, batteries and tyres, for all makes and models. The shop also arranges two-wheeler insurance and annual maintenance contracts, door to door, with pick-up and drop available. Its listing counts more than 3,000 happy customers so far - and this is a workshop that closes on Sundays.

Minor service,major repairs.

One counter for all of it. Pick a row and the write button carries your list into a ready email. Or just call — the phone is answered Monday to Saturday, 9am to 8pm.
