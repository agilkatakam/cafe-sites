A wrought-iron house of bread.

Francesco Carvalho started baking on Falkland Road in 1908. His goods reached American cruise ships so fast they nicknamed him American Express, and the name stayed. Around 1935 the bakery moved to Byculla, into AEB House — one of the few wrought-iron buildings left standing in Mumbai. The family still runs the ovens, five generations in.
