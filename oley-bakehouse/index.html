Baked by its owners

Oley is the dream of Vyomiini and Adithya, built from a shared love for the art and science of baking. From experimenting in small home kitchens to learning the craft professionally, every step shaped how they work: precision, creativity, and flavours that make people pause.

At Oley, baking is more than food. Each loaf, pastry and treat carries creativity, care and soulful ingredients. Not baking for the masses, but for meaning.

Vyomiini and Adithya, founders
