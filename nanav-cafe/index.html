An English breakfast cafe in Indiranagar, Bengaluru.

nanav

Brewed to perfection, served slow.

Hidden from the city's bustle, Nanav is a cosy spot for a classic English breakfast - eggs any style, sausages, baked beans and buttery toast, with freshly brewed coffee or tea.

Wooden decor, hanging greens and soft light set the tone upstairs. A little boutique of handcrafted goods keeps the ground floor.
