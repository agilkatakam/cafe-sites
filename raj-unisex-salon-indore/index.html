named Raj, in the city of the Rajwada

Indore's seven-storey Rajwada has stood at the middle of the city for generations. The salon on Kanadia Road shares the first three letters of its name, and aims at the same thing: people leaving looking their best.

It is a unisex salon, so the same door works for the whole family: a haircut for him, a facial for her, makeup for the wedding.
