A pure vegetarian kitchen

Deepee is a pure vegetarian restaurant in Baluganj, Agra, in the Meher Cinema Complex. Eat in, or carry out.

Its food is South Indian: the cuisine of dosas, idli and vada with sambar, banana-leaf thalis and filter coffee.

Every picture on this page is an archive illustration of the cuisine. None of them shows Deepee's own dining room or dishes.

Rice and lentils, soaked, ground and fermented, are where half of this cuisine begins.
