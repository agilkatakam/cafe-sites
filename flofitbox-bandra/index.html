Good to know

Is the first session really free?
Yes. FloFitBox offers a free, non-binding trial session, with or without one of our personal trainers. Call or WhatsApp +91 98199 69000 to book yours.
Do I need to be fit already?
No. Programmes scale to the person in front of the coach, from first-timers to experienced lifters. The trial session is the easiest way to see where you would start.
What can I train here?
Group classes, personal training, Gravity Pilates, weight lifting, mobility, boxing and FloFit Kids, plus open work on the turf floor.
Is there coaching, or do I train alone?
Both. You can book one-on-one personal training, join coached group sessions, or take your trial session with a trainer and decide from there.
Where exactly is FloFitBox?
33 Antoine Villa, ground floor, Sherly Mala Road, off Carter Road, Bandra West, Mumbai 400050.
What are your timings?
Class and floor timings change with the schedule, so call or WhatsApp +91 98199 69000 for this week's hours before you drop in.
How do I book?
The fastest way is WhatsApp or a call to +91 98199 69000. You can also email info@flofitbox.com.
