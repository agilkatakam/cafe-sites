Harpreet Confectioners has sold cakes from Booth 70 in Sector 22 since 1964. The board above the counter still says so.

The glass case is the menu: whole cakes, pastries by the piece, cupcakes and buns.

The shop puts it its own way: you can't buy happiness, but you can buy cakes, and that is kind of the same thing.

Open every day, 10 AM to 11 PM. Call the counter.
