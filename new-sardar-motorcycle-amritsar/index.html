in the shop

what the workshop does

fourteen checks and repairs, named on the listing. nothing here is a promise - the shop answers for its own work.

  • engine

    the heart of the bike, as the listing groups it.

    carburetor / oil filter / cylinder reboring and honing

  • electrical

    the wiring side, which its listing says it handles alongside the mechanical.

    battery repair / headlight repair / throttle action

  • running gear

    how the bike rolls and rides.

    wheel bearing play / spoke tension / rear suspension

  • fuel and air

    what goes in and what comes out.

    air cleaner / choke operation / drain tube

  • finish

    the look of the bike.

    parts painting / rubbing and polishing

its listing also mentions scooters, royal enfield bullet 350 cc and harley-davidson models, and complimentary estimates.
