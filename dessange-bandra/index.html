The house

In 1954, Jacques Dessange opened a small Paris salon with one conviction: hair should move. Seventy years on, his house practises that freedom on Turner Road, Bandra.

The Bandra salon. Savanna Court, 190 Turner Road, opposite Bandra House, Bandra West.

Before you visit

How long should I allow for a first visit?

Plan for about two hours. Every first appointment starts with a consultation before any service is confirmed.

Do you use products suited to Indian hair?

Yes. Paris methods, with products chosen for Indian hair density, texture and climate.

Can I request a specific artist?

Yes, you may choose your stylist when booking, subject to availability.

Do you offer bridal packages across functions?

Yes. The team plans across your functions, with trials and an on-location team where needed.

What are the hours?

Open every day, 10:00 to 21:00.
